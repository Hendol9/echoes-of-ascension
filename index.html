About this place

Echoes of Ascension is a short philosophical game about irreversibility. Every step forward erases the ground behind you. You can run, you can hesitate, but you cannot return.

Mechanically: it's a momentum platformer with coyote time, dash, and double-jump (earned). Emotionally: it's about the memories we collect not to keep, but to be shaped by.

There are 12 echoes hidden in 5 biomes: Roots, Hollow City, Crystal Veins, Storm Shelf, Veil. Each is a fragment of a letter you wrote to yourself before you started climbing. Built in a single HTML file for GitHub Pages. No trackers. No backend. Just you and the climb.
